# 6.4.2 Upcoming expenses view — prior art

Survey for the projection view: how apps present predicted next charges (calendar
vs list), the reference-date/"this month" window, overdue and gone-stale streams,
and what the "remaining" total means. Sources numbered; decisions at the end.

## Commercial apps — the feature in production

1. **Monarch Money — Recurring/Upcoming page**
   (help.monarch.com Tracking-Recurring-Expenses-and-Bills; monarch.com/blog
   track-recurring-bills-and-subscriptions): the closest match to this leaf's
   promise. One "Upcoming" page with **both a calendar and a list**; the list is
   split **Upcoming / Complete** for the viewed month, with month tabs to look
   ahead or back. Calendar entries are status-coded *with icons, not color
   alone*: paid as expected (green check), paid at a different amount (yellow
   check), **missed — due date passed with no matching transaction** (red X),
   upcoming (blue). Every sync re-scans for new recurring items; paychecks and
   transfers appear in the same calendar as bills.
2. **Rocket Money — Recurring tab** (help.rocketmoney.com
   managing-your-bills-and-subscriptions, where-can-i-view-my-subscriptions-and-bills,
   enabling-payday-view): default **Upcoming** view is a calendar plus the list of
   charges due soon (next ~2 weeks on mobile); **All** lists everything, with an
   **Inactive** section for streams "without detected payments in the past
   month" — dead streams are demoted, not projected. **Payday view** sums the
   bills due before the next paycheck into a safe-to-spend number: the
   remaining-total semantics ("what still leaves before X") in production.
3. **Copilot Money — "Expected this month"** (help.copilot.money Creating-Recurrings,
   Dashboard-Tab-Overview, macOS/iPad/Web articles): recurrings grouped **This
   Month / In the Future / Paused / Archived**; desktop/web adds an "Expected
   this month" section with per-item detail. Dashboard has a compact horizontal
   "Upcoming" strip. Expected-but-unposted recurrings are **excluded from the
   spending charts** — projections never mix into actuals.
4. **Quicken Simplifi — Projected Cash Flow** (support.simplifi.quicken.com
   Using-Projected-Cash-Flow; quicken.com/blog/quicken-calendar-view): recurring
   reminders drive a projected-balance chart and a calendar (actual balances for
   past dates, projected for future); overdue reminders get color cues plus
   text. Projections come only from recurring reminders and future-dated
   transactions — no statistical forecast of irregular spend.
5. **Lunch Money — month-scoped Recurring page** (support.lunchmoney.app
   the-basics-of-recurring, creating-recurring-items; lunchmoney.dev): "every
   month has its own Recurring Items page" — the view is **anchored to a month**,
   and a yearly item appears only in its renewal month. Status per item: linked
   to a transaction (check), **expected date passed with nothing linked
   (warning)**, expected date in the future (clock). Budgeting uses a **"Total
   expected recurring"** sum for the period. The API returns "recurring items to
   expect for a specified month" with per-item `occurrences` arrays — multiple
   expected dates per stream per month is a first-class shape.
6. **PocketGuard — "In My Pocket"** (clark.com, cnbc.com/select, thepennyhoarder.com
   reviews): headline number = income − **upcoming bills** − goals; upcoming
   recurring outflows are subtracted as money already spoken for. Reviews note
   the number is only as good as the detected bills — garbage-in warnings for
   stale detection.
7. **YNAB — scheduled transactions** (support.ynab.com
   scheduled-transactions-a-guide; eshmoneycoach.com toolkit
   show-upcoming-transaction-total): upcoming scheduled transactions render in
   the register but are **not counted in Activity until their date** — the
   projected/actual separation as doctrine. The community Toolkit's most-wanted
   patch adds exactly this leaf's number: the sum of upcoming transactions.

## Open source

8. **Actual Budget — schedules** (actualbudget.org/docs/schedules; PR #4815;
   releases 25.2.0, 26.3.0): schedule statuses are **missed / due / upcoming**,
   shown as icon+color chips in the budget table. The "upcoming length" window
   is user-configurable and grew an **"End of the current month"** option — the
   calendar-month horizon users actually asked for; 25.2.0 changed the window to
   show **all occurrences of a schedule within the period**, not just the next
   one; 26.3.0 fixed past missed dates leaking into "upcoming" (overdue and
   upcoming must be disjoint states).
9. **Firefly III — bills per period** (docs.firefly-iii.org subscriptions;
   issues #8195, #1841, #8058, discussion #9846): each bill shows "expected this
   period" / "paid this period" and a `next_expected_match`; the page is driven
   by an explicit **period range, not the wall clock** (changing the range
   changes what is "expected"). Issue #8058 requests the missing headline: a
   **sum of outstanding bills** for the period. Long bug history (#8195, #1841)
   shows next-date bookkeeping is where correctness dies — compute projections
   from history on read, don't store them.
10. **Maybe Finance** (github.com/maybe-finance/maybe issue #2091, discussion
    #1190): recurring forecasting never shipped, but the design threads agree on
    two rules adopted here: forecasted items must be **visually distinct from
    actuals** and must **never auto-convert into transactions**.

## Provider contract & data model

11. **Plaid Recurring Transactions** (plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/,
    plaid.com/blog/recurring-transactions/): the industry output shape this
    repo's 6.4.1 already mirrors — streams carry `predicted_next_date` and
    `is_active`; a stream that stops occurring goes inactive rather than
    projecting forever. Habitual purchases (coffee, gas) are deliberately not
    streams; ≥180 days of history recommended.
12. **Lunch Money API** (lunchmoney.dev, v1 recurring endpoint): recurring items
    are queried **for a month**, returning expected `occurrences` inside that
    month plus the nearest dates on either side — the projection is a pure
    function of (streams, month), which is exactly the clock-free contract the
    DAL needs.

## Calendar accessibility

13. **CSS-Tricks, "Making Calendars With Accessibility and Internationalization
    in Mind"** + **24a11y "A New Day: Making a Better Calendar"**: a read-only
    month view should be a real `<table>` — caption for the month, `<th
    scope="col">` weekday headers; screen-reader users navigate tables well.
    Don't strip table semantics with `role="presentation"`; don't use `<abbr>`
    day names. The ARIA `grid` pattern (roving tabindex, arrow keys,
    `aria-selected`) is for interactive *pickers*, not displays.
14. **W3C ARIA APG date-picker example + PatternFly calendar-month +
    Carnegie Museums a11y guidelines**: same table baseline; and offer a
    **chronological list alongside the grid** — for screen-reader and mobile
    users the list, not the grid, is the primary reading surface.
15. **Rob Berger, "My Favorite Calendar-Based Budgeting Apps"**
    (robberger.com/calendar-based-budgeting-apps): survey of calendar-first
    budgeting; the calendar's value is *when* money leaves, the list's value is
    *what* — apps that ship only one lose half the question.

## Decisions

**Adopted**
- **Month horizon, calendar + list** (1, 2, 5, 8, 13–15): the view is anchored
  to the reference date's calendar month — "still to leave this month" is the
  promise, Lunch Money scopes the whole page to a month, and Actual grew an
  end-of-month window by demand. Both surfaces ship: a read-only `<table>`
  month grid (caption + `scope="col"`, no fake ARIA grid) and the date-ordered
  list as the primary detail surface. Rocket's 2-week horizon rejected as
  arbitrary against the promise's own wording.
- **Explicit reference date, projection as a pure function** (9, 12): the DAL
  takes a reference date parameter; projection = f(streams, reference), computed
  on read, never stored (Firefly's stored-next-date bug tail). The page defaults
  to the request date and accepts a validated `?on=YYYY-MM-DD` override — the
  same month-navigation affordance Monarch/Lunch Money/Firefly expose, and the
  seam that keeps every test clock-free.
- **Overdue as its own labeled state, counted in the remaining total** (1, 5, 8):
  an occurrence expected before the reference date with no posted match is shown
  with an explicit "expected <date> — not seen" text label (icon/color never the
  only channel) and still counts as money to leave — Monarch's missed-X,
  Lunch Money's warning, Actual's missed chip; Payday-view/PocketGuard semantics
  count unpaid bills, not merely future ones.
- **Staleness bound: at most one missed occurrence projects** (2, 11): a stream
  whose second-next expected date is already past the reference date stops
  projecting (mirrors the detector's own one-missed-occurrence tolerance and
  Rocket's Inactive demotion / Plaid's `is_active`). Stale streams leave the
  calendar, list, and totals, and surface in an honest count note instead —
  with the seeded ledger ending March 2026, a far-future reference date shows
  "inactive" honesty, not months of phantom overdue bills.
- **All expected occurrences in the window, not just the next** (5, 8): weekly
  streams project every remaining date in the month (Lunch Money `occurrences`,
  Actual 25.2.0).
- **Dismissed out; proposed + confirmed project** (1, 2): Monarch/Rocket project
  detections immediately — confirmation refines, it doesn't gate. Dismissal is
  recorded user intent ("not recurring") and excludes the stream.
- **Outflows are the headline; inflows shown separately** (1, 2, 3): the
  remaining total sums expected outflows only ("still to leave"); expected
  deposits (paychecks — 6.4.1 detects them deliberately) render as their own
  quiet section and on the calendar with a plus sign, never netted against the
  charges total.
- **Per-currency totals, never combined** (repo law): sections per currency like
  /spending; no FX.
- **Projections never mix into actuals** (3, 7, 10): this page renders expected
  amounts from stream typicals; nothing here writes rows, and no ledger surface
  (cash flow, spending) counts a projection.

**Rejected**
- **Safe-to-spend / projected balances** (2, 4, 6): needs income timing + balance
  forecasting; out of scope (7.x territory), and Simplifi's community threads
  show the number misleads without complete recurring coverage.
- **Interactive calendar (ARIA grid, month paging widget)** (13, 14): a
  read-only table + the `?on=` seam gives month navigation without a focus-managed
  grid; 6.4.3's manager can grow interactivity if it earns it.
- **Marking occurrences paid / linking transactions to expected dates** (5, 9):
  Lunch Money/Firefly link transactions to expected occurrences; that's stateful
  matching machinery (and 6.4.3/3.2.1-adjacent). Here the detector's recompute
  already advances `lastDate` when a charge posts, which retires the projection
  by construction.
- **Early-detection (<3 occurrences) projection** (11): 6.4.1 deliberately
  rejected early streams; projecting them would resurrect the false-positive
  cost with money numbers attached.
- **Price-increase highlighting and annual-cost rollups** (2): named 6.4.3
  scope.
