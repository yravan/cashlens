# 2.2.3 Offline Accounts — Prior Art

> Track accounts with no feed — cash, prepaid balances, niche platforms like Kalshi — by periodically updating a balance.

Survey date: 2026-09-12. Design: [`2.2.3-offline-accounts-design.md`](./2.2.3-offline-accounts-design.md).

## Questions the survey had to answer

1. What does creating an offline account require, and which account types are offered?
2. Is the balance a figure the user reports, a figure derived from transactions, or both — and how do the two reconcile?
3. Does a balance update create a transaction, a snapshot, or neither?
4. How do rename, archive, close, and delete behave, and what happens to the account's transactions?
5. How do offline accounts interact with manual transactions and file imports?
6. How are provider-reported balances defined, so offline figures stay comparable?

### Assertions and anchors

| # | Source | What it does | Takeaway |
|---|--------|--------------|----------|
| 21 | hledger — [balance assertions](https://hledger.org/hledger.html#balance-assertions) | `=` asserts the running balance at a posting; checked in date order; mismatch is an error. | An asserted balance is a checkpoint, not an adjustment. |
| 22 | Beancount — [balance and pad](https://beancount.github.io/docs/beancount_language_syntax.html) | `balance` is asserted at the beginning of the day; `pad` inserts an adjusting transaction to make it hold. | Day-boundary semantics matter: "beginning of day" is a rule, not an accident. |
| 23 | Maybe / Sure — [account management](https://deepwiki.com/we-promise/sure/3-account-management) | Valuations `opening_anchor`, `current_anchor`, `reconciliation`; forward and reverse balance calculators walk transactions from an anchor. | The anchor-plus-ledger model this leaf ships. |
| 24 | Sure — [issue #3291](https://github.com/we-promise/sure/issues/3291) | Daily mid-day "manual balance update" anchors corrupt earlier balances: "the design is the bug". | An anchor needs an explicit day and a same-day tie-break, and must never rewrite the past. |

Also consulted, without a distinct pattern beyond the above: Copilot (transaction-driven balance rules per account type, edit balance), PocketSmith (calculated closing balance for offline accounts), Tiller (Balance History sheet with a manual-account update checkbox), Simplifi (Re-sync Balance; opening-balance edit for asset value).

## Patterns adopted

- **Anchor plus ledger** (Maybe/Sure, Lunch Money): the balance the user enters is an anchor; the shown balance is the anchor plus the account's posted transactions after it. Updating the balance re-anchors and never creates a transaction.
- **Explicit anchor day with a same-day tie-break** (Beancount's beginning-of-day rule, the Sure #3291 lesson): the anchor carries the user's local calendar day; rows dated after that day count, rows dated that day count only if they were recorded after the anchor instant, earlier rows never count.
- **Provider sign convention** (Plaid, 6.3.1): held types are positive when held, credit and loan are positive when owed; offline balances are entered in the same convention, so totals never mix conventions.
- **Balance-as-of disclosure** (Lunch Money `balance_as_of`): the row shows the reported figure, its day, and how many transactions moved it since.
- **Delete names its consequence** (Lunch Money, Actual): deleting an offline account states how many transactions go with it.
- **Minimal creation form** (Monarch, Empower): name, type, currency, balance.

## Patterns rejected for this leaf

- **Opening-balance transactions** (Firefly, GnuCash, Actual): a synthetic inflow would count as income in cash flow and spending; the anchor row already carries the figure.
- **Reconciliation adjustments** (YNAB, Actual): booking the difference as a transaction is a separate workflow and lands in 3.2.x territory.
- **Balance history and history editing** (Monarch, Lunch Money, Tiller): snapshots are 3.1.2; net worth over time is 6.3.2.
- **Per-account "transactions move the balance" toggle** (Monarch): one rule for every offline account is simpler and was the founder's choice.
- **Close/archive status** (Lunch Money `closed_on`, Actual `closeAccount`): deferred; delete covers the leaf's need.
- **Extra account types** (Lunch Money's real estate, vehicle, crypto): the ledger's five `account_type` values already cover the leaf's examples.
