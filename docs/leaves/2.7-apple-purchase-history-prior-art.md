# 2.7 - Apple purchase history: roadmap research

Research date: 2026-09-10. Scope: add Apple digital purchases and subscriptions
as a financial evidence source even without emailed receipts. This is roadmap
research, not a working connector or authorization to access an Apple Account.
P1 is proposed alongside receipt matching; the MVP path does not change.

## Survey

| # | Primary Apple source | Finding and roadmap consequence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [View purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) | Account holders can inspect purchase history and search charges. A bank descriptor is not the only available evidence. |
| 2 | [iPhone purchases and subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph4e3e7324f/ios) | Purchase history and subscription management expose different views. Preserve scope and dates; a current subscription list is not historical spending. |
| 3 | [Unrecognized Apple Services charges](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102396) | Apple Card charges can group purchases across days, without a separate receipt for each. Support many items per payment; never invent equal item amounts. |
| 4 | [Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105062) | Separate family accounts can share purchases. Do not assume the payer and purchaser are the same person. |
| 5 | [Family subscriptions and billing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) | Purchase sharing can charge the organizer. Preserve uncertain attribution rather than assigning every item to the cardholder. |
| 6 | [Hidden purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108091) | Hiding an app does not erase complete purchase history. A visible purchased-app list does not prove complete coverage. |
| 7 | [Data and Privacy tools](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102283) | Apple offers account-associated data copies; certain scheduled requests are regional. Evaluate a user-mediated export path and disclose regional/refresh limitations. |
| 8 | [App Store privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/) | Account-linked purchase data exists, but a privacy disclosure is not a third-party authorization API. Import only selected financial evidence. |
| 9 | [App Store Server API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreserverapi/) | Developer transaction/subscription APIs concern purchases in the developer's app, not an established account-wide consumer connector. |
| 10 | [Get Transaction History](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreserverapi/get-transaction-history) | App-scoped transaction history does not establish access to purchases from unrelated apps. Reject that shortcut for Cash Lens. |
| 11 | [App Store Server Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/AppStoreServerNotifications) | Developer notifications concern their in-app purchase lifecycle. They are not evidence of a consumer-account-wide feed. |
| 12 | [Sign in with Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/SigninwithApple) | Authentication is distinct from purchase-history authorization. Do not present an Apple sign-in button as permission to read purchases. |
| 13 | [Manage Sign in with Apple apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102571) | Users can manage/revoke sign-in relationships. Any future source connection needs equally clear consent and removal controls. |

The [Data & Privacy file-guide entry point](https://privacy.apple.com/file-guides/transfer/appdata)
is a further implementation research lead, not a validated parser contract:
its document/schema content was not available through this audit's text fetch.
No export file, credentials, or real purchase records were collected.

## Decisions and feasibility gate

Adopt Apple's own purchase history and user-requested data as candidate source
evidence, not dependence on Gmail. An explicit user-mediated export/import path
is a plausible first implementation; it is not continuous synchronization.
Before building, verify the actual authorized acquisition route, sample format,
available item/price/currency/identifier fields, historical coverage, regional
eligibility, and whether repeat access is supported. Document missing fields.
The sources reviewed do not establish a universal third-party Apple Account
purchase API; this is not a claim that no other eligible transfer route exists.

The outcome remains identifying the purchases behind Apple or PayPal charges.
Do not reduce it to labeling the merchant "Apple" or importing a bare statement.
Use existing 3.3.2 cross-source merging, 3.3.3 receipt matching, and 3.4.1 splits
to relate source items to payments once those leaves are available. Show
unmatched/grouped/partial records and seek review when attribution is uncertain.
One bank debit plus one PayPal record plus Apple items must not become three
separate expenses. Refund and family-purchaser relationships require evidence.

This leaf concerns digital purchase/subscription history, not Apple Store
hardware order tracking or Cash Lens's own future App Store subscription backend.
No automatic Apple login, password/2FA collection, session-cookie extraction,
or undocumented credential-scraping connector is approved. Export archives can
contain unrelated personal information: require explicit selection, bounded
parsing, minimal retention, protected storage, redacted logs, owner isolation,
and deletion controls. Source documents remain untrusted input to any model.

## Delivery and verification when built

Confirm an agent-sized 1-3 PR split before implementation: authorized intake and
preview; normalized item/provenance import with repeat-import safety; and the
payment-match/review experience using existing ledger primitives. If access or
matching requires a larger change, split the leaf before coding rather than
quietly expanding it. Do not promise background refresh until its route is proven.

Use sanitized, consented format samples and synthetic fixtures for a grouped
bill spanning dates, missing email receipts, a subscription renewal, a refund,
a family purchase, duplicate export uploads, and unmatched/partial records.
Assert exact per-currency totals and retained source links, not just successful
parsing. Test hostile/oversized archives, unsupported schema versions, malformed
amounts, deletion, and cross-user isolation. Mutation tests must catch duplicate
counting and invented item allocation. No provider connection is implemented or
certified by this documentation change.
