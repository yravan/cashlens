# 6.3.1 Accounts Overview — Prior Art

Survey date: 2026-08-27

Scope: account grouping, asset/liability balance semantics, current versus available balances, stale/disconnected state, multi-currency totals without implicit FX, empty/loading/error behavior, privacy, and mobile/accessibility for the Cash Lens accounts overview.

## Sources and decisions

| # | Source | Evidence | Cash Lens decision |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Plaid Accounts API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/accounts/) | Plaid defines `depository`, `credit`, `loan`, `investment`, and `other`; subtype is nullable. `current` is funds held or debt outstanding, so positive credit/loan current means owed. `available`, `current`, and `limit` can be null. `/accounts/get` is cached; `/accounts/balance/get` is the live endpoint. | Adopt provider types as inputs, stable product groups as output, and Plaid-reported current semantics unchanged. Never imply a missing balance is zero. Do not claim institution-level freshness beyond the connection status already modeled. |
| 2 | [Monarch getting started](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048393272-Getting-Started-Guide) | Monarch makes Accounts a first-class destination for balances, activity, and account detail. | Adopt a scan-first account destination. Reject activity/history here because that is leaf 3.6.1. |
| 3 | [Monarch credit utilization](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/39587584456596-Tracking-Credit-Utilization) | Credit accounts separate balance owed, limit, remaining credit, and utilization. | Adopt explicit “credit owed” language. Defer utilization and limits because 6.3.1 promises balance totals, not credit analytics. |
| 4 | [YNAB account types](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/account-types-an-overview-BkmGM0qCq) | YNAB uses coarse user-facing groups (Budget, Loan, Tracking) rather than exposing every institution subtype. | Adopt coarse, stable groups with provider subtype as secondary text. Do not expose Plaid enum vocabulary as the primary information architecture. |
| 5 | [YNAB reconciliation](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/getting-started-with-reconciling-accounts-an-overview-Sy3JWx4Js) | YNAB distinguishes cleared, uncleared, and working balances because each answers a different question. | Adopt one clearly labeled source-of-truth balance. Reject mixing transaction-derived and provider-reported balances in this leaf. |
| 6 | [YNAB glossary](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/ynab-glossary-a-guide-BJd80SORq) | Credit-card balance and the amount reserved to pay it are separate concepts. | Show what is owed, not “available to pay”; budgeting/payment-reserve concepts are out of scope. |
| 7 | [Quicken Simplifi supported account types](https://support.simplifi.quicken.com/en/articles/6060379-what-account-types-are-supported-in-quicken-simplifi) | Simplifi groups a long subtype list under Banking, Investments, Property/Assets, and Loans/Debt. | Adopt Cash, Credit, Loans, Investments, and Other as stable groups; retain subtype as supporting context. |
| 8 | [Simplifi balance discrepancies](https://support.simplifi.quicken.com/en/articles/4202003-how-to-resolve-balance-discrepancies-in-quicken-simplifi) | Simplifi makes “bank balance” versus “balance with pending” an explicit user choice. | Reject silently combining pending transactions with provider current balance. The overview displays the stored provider balance only. |
| 9 | [Simplifi credit-card details](https://support.simplifi.quicken.com/en/articles/14624970-using-credit-card-details) | Card detail can include statement balance, minimum due, due date, used credit, limit, and available credit. | Adopt debt-specific wording. Defer statement/due/limit fields because Cash Lens does not store them for this leaf. |
| 10 | [Simplifi savings goals](https://support.simplifi.quicken.com/en/articles/3676756-using-quicken-simplifi-s-savings-goals) | Simplifi distinguishes bank balance from spendable balance after goals. | Reject invented “spendable” calculations. Cash on hand is the sum of current depository balances only. |
| 11 | [Actual Budget accounts](https://actualbudget.org/docs/accounts/) | Actual separates budgeted and tracking accounts; closed accounts remain visible in a dedicated group and closure is reversible. | Adopt clear grouping and honest retained data. For Cash Lens, disconnected accounts with retained ledger data remain visible; purged accounts disappear through existing behavior. |
| 12 | [Actual Budget starting fresh](https://actualbudget.org/docs/getting-started/starting-fresh/) | Checking, savings, and active cards are typically active/budgeted; investments, loans, retirement, and tracked assets form distinct roles. | Adopt asset-versus-liability mental models while keeping visible groups concrete. Reject net-worth aggregation because it is leaf 6.3.2. |
| 13 | [Actual Budget multi-currency](https://actualbudget.org/docs/budgeting/multi-currency/) | Actual documents conversion as an explicit, rate-bearing workaround; unlike currencies cannot be meaningfully totaled without FX. | Adopt per-currency totals and reject implicit FX or a single mixed-currency grand total. |
| 14 | [Actual Budget API reference](https://actualbudget.org/docs/api/reference/) | Balance queries are account-specific and can be evaluated at a date; initial/current balance provenance matters. | Keep the overview on the latest stored balance row per account. Defer historical/as-of balances to balance history. |
| 15 | [W3C accessible tables tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/tables/) | Tables require semantic headers, captions, and row/column associations; layout tables are inappropriate. | Prefer grouped semantic lists/cards for the small mobile-first account summary; if using a grid, preserve native relationships rather than ARIA imitation. |
| 16 | [WCAG 2.2 reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow) | At 320 CSS px, independent summaries should stack; no account detail/action should disappear. Horizontal scrolling is reserved for genuinely two-dimensional data. | Use stacked account rows/cards and keep connection actions available on mobile. |
| 17 | [WCAG 2.2 status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) | Loading completion and refreshed totals should be announced without moving focus, using a contextual live status where updates occur. | Use meaningful server loading/error states and preserve focus for client-driven connection/sync actions. |
| 18 | [WCAG use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/use-of-color.html) | Meaning cannot be conveyed through color alone. | Pair all debt/connection states with text labels; color remains supplemental. |

Sources 2–10 were verified through their indexed first-party documentation summaries; Monarch’s migrated help host returned 403 to direct fetch. Sources 1 and 11–18 were fetched directly from primary documentation.

## Adopted pattern

- Two top-level per-currency summaries: **Cash on hand** (depository accounts) and **Credit owed** (credit accounts). No cross-currency total and no FX.
- Every retained account shown exactly once in a stable group: Cash, Credit, Loans, Investments, Other. Provider subtype is secondary context.
- Provider-reported current balance displayed verbatim under existing sign semantics: depository positive means held; credit/loan positive means owed. Missing balance is displayed as unavailable, never `$0`.
- Existing connection state and controls remain the source for importing/repair/disconnect/purge status. Disconnected accounts with retained data remain visible; purged accounts naturally disappear.
- Mobile-first grouped list with textual state labels, semantic headings, and no color-only meaning.

## Rejected or deferred

- Implicit exchange rates, mixed-currency grand totals, or net worth (6.3.2).
- Available balance, statement balance, credit utilization, payment due, limits, or transaction-derived balance reconstruction: not reliably stored by this leaf.
- Activity, history, search, and filters (3.6.1 and later).
- Manual grouping/editing and offline account management (2.2.3 / later product work).
