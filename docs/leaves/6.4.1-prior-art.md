# 6.4.1 Recurring charge detection — prior art

Survey for the detector (grouping, cadence, amount tolerance, thresholds) and the
confirm/dismiss lifecycle. Sources numbered; adopted/rejected notes at the end.

## Provider prior art

1. **Plaid Recurring Transactions** (`/transactions/recurring/get`, docs + blog:
   plaid.com/docs/transactions/, plaid.com/blog/recurring-transactions/,
   plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/). Groups a user's history into inflow
   and outflow *streams* "based on the description, amount, and cadence". A stream
   is **mature at ≥3 occurrences**; below that it is early-detection only. Frequency
   enum: `WEEKLY / BIWEEKLY / SEMI_MONTHLY / MONTHLY / ANNUALLY / UNKNOWN`. Each
   stream carries `average_amount`, `last_amount`, `last_date`, `predicted_next_date`,
   `is_active`, `status`. Plaid recommends **≥180 days of history** for good results.
   Their model is ML; the shape of the output (stream = merchant grouping + cadence
   class + typical amount + last seen + next predicted) is the industry contract.

## Open-source implementations

2. **Actual Budget — "Find schedules"**
   (`packages/loot-core/src/server/schedules/find-schedules.ts`). Fully rule-based:
   candidate cadence configs (weekly, every-2-weeks, monthly-by-day, monthly-last-day,
   1st/3rd and 2nd/4th weekday-of-month) are slid one day at a time over scan windows;
   each candidate generates **exactly 3 expected occurrence dates** and requires a
   transaction with the **identical payee** within **±2 days** of every occurrence,
   amount within `getApproxNumberThreshold(amount)` (≈7.5%). Candidates are ranked
   `sum(1/(dayDiff+1))`, grouped by payee, best rank wins. Monthly start days > 28
   are skipped ("28 is the max number of days all months are guaranteed to have").
   Result becomes a schedule with `is` / `isapprox` conditions on date and amount.
